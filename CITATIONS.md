# Citation identities and method attribution

The complete bibliography was recovered from the local bilingual manuscript package dated 12 October 2026. This revision did not find a missing bibliography in that package. It preserves its fixed source links and most bibliographic metadata. Primary records and relevant original source sections were checked for the additions below; this is not a new audit of every page of every cited work.

| Source | Appropriate use in this manuscript | Boundary |

The original Sections 2-3 had no external method citations despite substantial method inheritance. The revision adds citations near the positive lift, normal-ordered square, compressed exchanges, coherent occupation and thermal comparison; it retains the manuscript's proofs for the geometric bridges. No claim of being first is made.

The [Shen contribution statement](https://github.com/Shencongli/bosonic-laughlin-spectral-gap/blob/main/AI_CONTRIBUTIONS.md) was read as a disclosure example, not as verification evidence for this manuscript. The current bibliography distinguishes that statement from the fixed v0.1 mathematical source.

The old bibliography's fixed commits identify the mathematical inputs. Longer dependency identifiers needed for a later Lean release are recorded separately in VERIFICATION.md. No private source URLs containing temporary credentials are retained.
